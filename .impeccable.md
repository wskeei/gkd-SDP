## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are Android users trying to reduce compulsive phone usage inside a tool-heavy self-discipline app. They are typically entering this feature in the middle of real distraction pressure, not in a relaxed browsing context, so the interface needs to help them make deliberate choices quickly and with low ambiguity.

Their main job is to keep protective rules active, selectively allow app use when necessary, and review their own usage behavior without getting lost in bloated settings screens.

### Brand Personality
Voice and tone should feel disciplined, intentional, and trustworthy.

Three-word personality:

- restrained
- vigilant
- ceremonial

Emotional goal:

- emphasize constraint, reminder, and ritual rather than playful productivity or generic utility

### Aesthetic Direction
The design should stay within Android conventions, but feel more custom and layered than stock Material scaffolding. Favor stronger grouping, tighter information density, and more intentional hierarchy over long stacked forms.

Reference direction:

- high-quality Android apps that respect Material behavior but add more considered card structure, spacing rhythm, and visual hierarchy

Anti-direction:

- bloated debug-panel layouts
- repetitive full-width forms with weak grouping
- cheap-looking utility UI that feels patched together

### Design Principles
1. Make protection feel deliberate. Controls around self-discipline should feel intentional and a little ceremonial, not casual.
2. Reduce vertical bloat. Prefer grouped sections, icon-led scanning, and compact high-signal layouts over endless stacked rows.
3. Keep the primary action path calm and constrained. Preset choices should lead; secondary or custom actions should stay available but visually deferred.
4. Favor clear Android-native behavior with custom hierarchy. Stay compatible with platform expectations while improving density, contrast, and structure.
5. Treat feedback as part of trust. Saves, mode changes, and record filtering should always provide clear confirmation or empty-state guidance.
